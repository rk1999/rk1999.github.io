Driftbloom — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026 · Publisher: Rishabh Kumrawat · Contact: rishabh.k1999@gmail.com

Driftbloom is a game made by Rishabh Kumrawat ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the game handles. I do not run any servers for Driftbloom and I do not collect your personal information myself.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (stars, best scores, chosen pond theme, sound setting and whether you bought "remove ads") is saved only on your device. It is never sent to me. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Android version: advertising (Google AdMob)

The free Android version shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch to continue a night, and an occasional full-screen ad between nights. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time with the privacy settings button on the title screen. On any Android device you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Android version: purchases (Google Play)

The optional "remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. I never see your payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether the purchase is owned, so that ads stay turned off.

Windows and Linux versions

The desktop versions contain no ads, no analytics and make no network connections. The store you downloaded the game from (for example itch.io or Steam) has its own privacy policy.

Children

Driftbloom is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly allow ads to be personalised for them. If you believe a child has used the game and want information removed, contact me and I will help.

Your rights

Because I do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data should be made to Google using the links above. For any other question about this policy, email rishabh.k1999@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
